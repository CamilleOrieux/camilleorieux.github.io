Camille Orieux

Disrupted

Edition of a plastic experiment based on the collection of TV program guide magazines (spanning all eras and editions) in order to create a general caricature.The layout grid is disrupted into a mix of content from different programs. By leaving empty spaces, this approach subverts the aesthetic of a strictly ordered and traditionally arranged page that we are used to seeing in TV program magazines. By challenging expectations, it denounces the visual standardization aimed at the general public.
