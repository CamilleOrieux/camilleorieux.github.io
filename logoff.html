Camille Orieux

Log off

A communication campaign displayed within a high school to encourage students to reflect on the LOG OFF movement and question digital practices. A representation of the irresistible appeal of social media : a personified fish that embodies the curious and spontaneous user, caught up in the digital tide. The deliberately ironic montage highlights the absurdity and makes the campaign more relatable.

Animation for this paired project, created by Eva Tuyeres
