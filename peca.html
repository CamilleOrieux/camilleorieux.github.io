Peccadilles

Peccadilles is a food brand based on edible insects. Its goal is to become part of the general public’s everyday diet.
In this context, using the character of the foodie helps associate edible insects with a world of pleasure and taste experimentation.
The childlike and simple tone makes the experience feel lighter, more fun, and encourages people to try it “just to see.”
