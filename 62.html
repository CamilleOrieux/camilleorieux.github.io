62 offshore wind turbines

To reflect on our relationship with nature, the images that came to my mind were of the underwater disturbances caused by the installation of 62 offshore wind turbines, and the impact of electromagnetic fields on marine flora. In contrast to seaweed usually swaying freely and gracefully, I pictured them frozen and oddly shaped as if they had been electrocuted by the currents.
